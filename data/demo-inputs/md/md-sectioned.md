# Yard Traffic Rules

## Overview
The yard is shared by trailers, forklifts, and pedestrians moving between buildings. All yard traffic follows the painted lane markings and posted signage.

## Speed Limits
Vehicles are limited to 15 km/h in the yard and 5 km/h within 10 meters of any building entrance. Speed limits are enforced regardless of whether the yard is busy or empty.

## Right of Way
Trailers backing into a dock always have right of way over other yard traffic. Forklifts and other vehicles must stop and wait until the trailer has finished backing in before crossing behind it.

## Blind Spot Awareness
Drivers must assume they cannot be seen by trailer or crane operators near the loading docks. Pedestrians should make eye contact with an operator before crossing in front of any moving vehicle.
